# Manuscript and bibliography revision — 4 November 2026

The revised author reading PDF separates improvements in task execution from reuse of an edited improvement mechanism and from evidence of more effective subsequent optimization. A new table proposes comparisons for task performance, artifact transfer, recursive reuse, optimization efficiency and continual adaptation. These are reporting criteria, not experiments conducted by this survey. The abstract, conclusion and limitations now make these boundaries explicit.

The manuscript references companion dataset **v0.4.1**: 145 category–source records, 113 works, 60 AI-assisted primary-source checks, and 45 scoped observations across 43 records. Seventeen checked records have no coded outcome; 85 records remain inherited, and none are independently human-adjudicated. This editorial revision adds no dataset records or literature entries and does not advance the search horizon.
